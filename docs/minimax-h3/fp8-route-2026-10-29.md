# The release's own FP8 route: the four projections in W8A8 E4M3 (2026-10-29)

The DiT is at its BF16 ceiling - 724.5 TFLOP per evaluation at 87.6 TFLOPS,
73% of the 2.45 GHz `mma.sync` peak (`dit-gemm-shape-2026-10-29.md`) - and every
other kernel is within about 20% of its own floor. The one lever left with a
measured price is the release's: it converts the projection weights
to E4M3 at load and runs them W8A8 (`fp8-projections-2026-10-29.md`). This is
that route, ported behind `H3_DIT_GEMM=bf16|fp8`, default `bf16`, and measured
in both arms.

## 1. The reference

FastVideo at the revision the release pins (`configs/dependencies.json`,
`3d8ac9d14bd697a89ede8f170cbfbca012a9edcc`), file
`fastvideo/layers/quantization/fp8_config.py`:

- `_FP8_SUFFIXES` and `FP8Config.get_quant_method` tag `ffn.fc_in`, `ffn.fc_out`,
  `to_q`, `to_k`, `to_v`, `to_out`; `runtime/stage1_ops/lookup.py:15-16` is the
  release's own copy of that suffix list, and `runtime/stage1.py:126-134` tags
  every matching `LinearBase` with `FP8Config()` + `FP8QuantizeMethod("tensor")`
  and calls `convert_model_to_fp8`.
- Weight, tensorwise: `amax` over the whole tensor, `nan_to_num`,
  `scale = (amax / 448).clamp(min = 1 / (448 * 512))`, then
  `(w / scale.to(w.dtype)).clamp(-448, 448).to(float8_e4m3fn)` - the divisor and
  the quotient both rounded to the operand's own dtype.
- Activation, tensorwise: the same three steps on the 2-D view.
- `convert_model_to_fp8` drops the BF16 parameter it converted
  (`mod._parameters.pop("weight")`), so the FP8 model is smaller than its
  source.
- The matmul is `torch._scaled_mm(x_fp8, w_fp8.t(), scale_a, scale_b,
  out_dtype=torch.bfloat16)`: E4M3 x E4M3, F32 accumulate, BF16 output.

## 2. What was ported

`native/h3/h3_dit_gpu.{cu,h}`:

- `H3D_FP8`, a one-byte dtype. `h3d_is` still matches a dtype exactly, so no
  existing op can be handed one.
- `h3d_fp8_amax_kernel` (block reduction, `atomicMax` on non-negative floats),
  `h3d_fp8_scale_kernel` (`fmaxf(amax / 448, 1 / (448 * 512))`),
  `h3d_fp8_quantize_kernel` (the BF16-rounded divisor, the BF16-rounded
  quotient, the clamp, `__nv_cvt_float_to_fp8(..., __NV_E4M3)`).
- `h3d_fp8_scale` and `h3d_fp8_quantize` wrap those two steps;
  `h3d_fp8_quantize` takes the range explicitly, because the four projections
  share one activation scratch.
- `h3d_linear_e4m3`: `h3d_linear_lt`'s configuration (row-major, TRANSB, F32
  compute, BF16 output) with E4M3 operands and the two tensorwise scales as
  `CUBLASLT_MATMUL_DESC_A_SCALE_POINTER` / `_B_SCALE_POINTER`, which the header
  documents as taking the compute type. alpha stays 1, so no projection needs a
  host round trip.

`crates/ds4-core/src/minimax_h3/dit.rs`: `gemm_fp8_from_env` reads
`H3_DIT_GEMM` once per load; the loader converts each resident block's four
matrices at load (`fp8_matrix`, then the BF16 tensor is dropped, as the
reference drops its parameter); `projection` is the one place the four calls
happen, BF16 or FP8. `Dtype::Fp8` joins the sys layer at code 3, one byte per
element.

One limit, named: `H3_DIT_GEMM=fp8` with `--ssd-streaming` is refused
(the ring's slots are BF16 and refilled per block).

## 3. The defect the reproducer found

The model failed twice, first with an illegal device access then with a host
heap corruption, and both were the same mistake: `h3d_linear_e4m3` passed the
`h3d_tensor` structs where `cublasLtMatmul` wants the data pointers. It was
found by `tests/h3/fp8_gemm_bm`, which prices the route in isolation and was
given the four ways of handing cuBLASLt the scales (`local_gemm`): alpha 1 with
no scales, the two scale-pointer attributes, the scales folded into a host
alpha, and the scales folded into a device alpha under device pointer mode.

At the QKV projection's shape (18,799 x 5,376 x 21,504) all three real variants
pass, at 3.4% relative RMS against the BF16 path - the E4M3 noise - while the
production entry point crashed in the same binary, which is what localized the
defect to the call's arguments rather than the scaling scheme. The scale-pointer
form - the reference's own - was kept. The same reproducer gates the scheme
itself: at 7,680 x 5,376 x 21,504 the host implementation of
`fp8_config.py`'s two steps reports the scale exact (rel 0) and **0 of 22,020,096
activation bytes and 0 of 115,605,504 weight bytes off by more than half an
E4M3 ulp**.

## 4. The measurements

Job A: 768x768, 107 frames, four evaluations, `H3_DIT_SOL=0.9`, one binary,
`sm_121a`. Per evaluation, from a differenced pair of `--denoise` captures
(two and four evaluations) per arm, clocks 2,405 MHz mode, power 90.47 W peak:

| kernel | calls | BF16 ms/eval | FP8 ms/eval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `cutlass_80_tensorop_bf16_s16816gemm` (qkv/out/fc1/fc2) | 150 | 7,425 | - |
| `nvjet_sm121_tst_mma_128x240x64` (out) | 50 | 800 | - |
| `nvjet_sm121_qqtst_mma_128x256x128` | 100 | - | 2,675 |
| `nvjet_sm121_qqtst_mma_192x176x128` | 100 | - | 1,128 |
| `h3d_fp8_amax_kernel` (activation) | 200 | - | 611 |
| `h3d_fp8_quantize_kernel` (activation) | 200 | - | 503 |
| `h3d_qkv_rope_kernel` | 50 | 430 | 441 |
| `h3d_adaln_kernel` | 102 | 217 | 216 |
| `h3d_sol_forward_kernel` | 50 | 1,300 | 1,311 |
| `h3d_sol_vsa_kernel` | 50 | 219 | 212 |
| `h3d_sol_pool_kernel` | 50 | 134 | 126 |
| `h3d_sol_threshold_kernel` | 50 | 68 | 69 |
| `h3d_gate_kernel` | 100 | 325 | 302 |
| `h3d_swiglu_kernel` | 50 | 369 | 380 |
| `h3d_patch_convert_kernel` | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| **total** | | **11,289** | **7,977** |

The projections fall from 8,225 ms to 3,803, the two new activation passes add
1,114, and the net is **-3,312 ms per evaluation, 29.3%**. Everything else is
unchanged within a few milliseconds, which is the cross-check that the arm
changes one thing. The FP8 arm's own differenced total is 7,982 ms, 5 ms from
the sum of its rows.

The BF16 column is built from the per-call rates both captures of its pair
agree on, because in two independent runs the steps=4 process of the BF16 arm
ran `h3d_swiglu_kernel` at 72.2 and 74.9 ms per call against 7.32 and 7.38 in
its own steps=2 process - a 10x anomaly in one kernel of one capture, not
present in the FP8 arm's captures (380 ms per evaluation, 7.6 ms per call).
Its projections, qkv_rope, AdaLN, sol_forward, VSA, pool, threshold and gate
were all within 1% between the two captures. The record's own table
(`denoise-attribution-2026-10-28.md`, corrected) gives 11,282 ms with the same
per-kernel rates, which is the second check on the column.

The plain gate, one process per arm: BF16 `FORWARD video=b0150d3a0eebf5a8`
in 13.672 s, FP8 `FORWARD video=cb35717161ef557f` in 7.872 s. The hashes differ,
as they must: this is different arithmetic, and the render is the quality gate.

## 5. The render, and what the arm costs in quality

Job A's render in both arms, one process each, the fast arm's NVFP4 encoder
(`--text-nvfp4 qwen3vl_32b_minimax_h3_nvfp4_awq.safetensors`),
`H3_VAE_ATTENTION=tensor H3_VAE_GEMM=bf16`:

| arm | MP4 sha256 | bytes | `LOAD` | `DENOISE` | `FRAMES` |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BF16 | `5b5e735c1ebcbf2e99abd3a69f210affd0844406c473bdedca707cabeb224876` | 2,949,703 | 32.807 | 54.932 | 38.974 |
| FP8 | `4611c9478c00ee28c159e04d9a057ee19bb08fe827aad7d79ef2f855392532aa` | 2,626,007 | 21.693 | 31.891 | 34.173 |

The BF16 arm's MP4 is **byte-identical to the campaign's own** (`5b5e735c...`),
which is the proof that the refactor and the new switch change nothing on the
default path. The two arms' walls are not comparable across rows: the BF16
render paid 32.8 s of `LOAD` against the FP8 arm's 21.7, which is this host's
page-cache regime, not the arm.

Per-pixel, decoded yuv420p over all 107 frames: **luma RMS 20.919, worst pixel
150; chroma RMS 8.400, worst 106**, worst on the last frames (frame 106:
RMS 30.15). Side by side at frame 53 the composition is the same - the fox, the
sun, the trees - and the FP8 frame is visibly softer: the fine texture of the
fur, the snow and the background branches is smoothed away. That is the cost of
the arm at this shape and step count, and it is what the operator has to weigh
against 29.3% of every evaluation. FastVideo's per-row (`granularity="channel"`)
activation scales are the documented alternative and are not measured here.

## 6. What is left

- The two activation passes are 1,114 ms of the 4,423 the arm gives back, and
  they read each activation twice (amax, then quantize). A fused single pass
  would be an invented deviation from the reference's two steps; the reference's
  `quantize_input` reuses one quantization across q/k/v, which this port already
  gets for free because its qkv is one GEMM.
- Per-row activation scales and per-channel weight scales (`granularity
  ="channel"`) are untried; that arm is the one to measure if the softening is
  not acceptable.
- The streaming ring and every other family kernel stay BF16.
- The quality gate the campaign would want next is a teacher-forced
  logit/NLL comparison against the BF16 arm over many positions, not single
  frontiers (`AGENTS.md`, "Frontier logits are chaotic").

## 7. Reproducing

    make CUDA_ARCH=sm_121 h3-rs tests/h3_dit_gate tests/h3/fp8_gemm_bm
    tests/h3/fp8_gemm_bm 18799 5376 21504 0        # the route, checked
    tests/h3/fp8_gemm_bm 512 1024 2048 3           # scale pointers, variant 1
    H3_DIT_GEMM=fp8 tests/h3_dit_gate <text_encoder> <tokenizer.json> \
        <FastH3-4step transformer> "<prompt>" --width 768 --height 768 \
        --frames 107 --steps 4
    H3_DIT_SOL=0.9 H3_DIT_GEMM=fp8 H3_VAE_ATTENTION=tensor H3_VAE_GEMM=bf16 \
        ./h3-rs -d <FastH3-4step> --text-nvfp4 <nvfp4 awq> -p "<prompt>" \
        --width 768 --height 768 --frames 107 --steps 4 --seed 42 -o out.mp4

The nsys pairs live at `~/fp8-{bf16,fp8}-{2,4}.nsys-rep` on the Spark (plus
`~/fp8-bf16b-{2,4}` for the second BF16 pair), the pair tables at
`~/fp8-nsys-table.txt`, the two renders and their digests at
`~/fp8-render-{bf16,fp8}.{mp4,txt}`, and the harnesses at `~/fp8-{gate,nsys,
tail,variants,variants2}.sh`.
