# DeepSeek V4.1 indexer score: two tensor-core arms and the served default

2026-11-08. Long-context prefill on one Spark spends most of its time in a
single kernel: the lightning indexer's score. This page records what was
tried, why the arms are shaped the way they are, and what is measured. The
data behind the numbers is the
[2K-64K three-arm record](benchmarks/ds41-indexer-arm-2026-11-08/README.md);
this page is the reasoning and the rounds.

## The cost this targets

The lightning indexer scores every prompt row against every earlier source
group, for four of the 40 layers at ratio 2 and one at ratio 1. It is the only
term of prefill that grows with the square of the context. On the scalar
kernel its cost tracks instruction issue rather than arithmetic throughput:
in the record the scalar arm loses 6.6% of its prefill rate between 2K and
64K while the tensor-core arms hold their level, so the gap widens from
+0.9% to +23.8% over the same range. Threads and occupancy cannot move an
issue-bound kernel; only the tensor cores can, at the cost of an accumulation
order that is no longer element-identical.

## Arm 1: the s8 MMA

The index q is `act_quant` mode 1 output (e2m1 x ue8m0 per 32 dims,
`cuda/ds41_attn.cuh`); the index key cache stores MXFP4 e2m1 x E8M0 per 32
dims (`ds41_kvfmt.h`). Both mantissas times 2 are integers of magnitude at
most 12, so an `m16n8k32` int8 MMA accumulates each 32-dim block exactly in
int32 (|sum| <= 32 x 144) and the block scale 2^(eq+ek-2) is exact. The four
block sums are added as (T0+T1)+(T2+T3): one rounding point, where the scalar
kernel folds lane-in 4 dims and then runs an xor butterfly across blocks.

The port is `cuda/ds41_indexer_mma.cuh`, with the prep kernel, the templated
score kernel (head count 32 or 64, dim 128), the shared q scratch and the
dispatch in `cuda/ds41_indexer.cuh`. Deviations, named in the header:

- one q scratch slot instead of a per-lane array; the port has no decode-lane
  concept yet, the same deviation the candidate scratch already records;
- a grid.y overflow falls back to the scalar kernel (which wraps by grid
  stride) instead of refusing the forward; unreachable below roughly 134M
  groups;
- a block whose amax is beneath 2^-122 is zeroed: beneath that the block scale
  is not a representable normal f32 (0.0f at exponent -127, -inf beneath), so
  no arithmetic exists to reproduce.

## Arm 2: native MXFP4 block scaling

The indexer operands are already MXFP4, and PTX ISA 9.5 defines
`mma.sync.aligned.kind::mxf4.block_scale.scale_vec::2X.m16n8k64` with
hardware block scaling on `sm_120a`+ (compile-probed on `sm_121a`). One
instruction then covers 64 dims, two 32-dim blocks with their own ue8m0
scales, so the nibble LUT and the explicit scale multiplies disappear and one
instruction carries 8192 MACs instead of 4096.

Two pieces make it safe:

- a prep kernel that re-derives `act_quant`'s own power-of-two block scale
  from the stored f32 q (`s = pow2_ceil_log2(max(amax, 6*2^-126)/6)`,
  `cuda/ds41_attn.cuh`) and repacks the e2m1 nibbles; a block whose largest
  nibble is 3 may re-derive half the scale and double every nibble, which is
  the same value on the e2m1 grid, so the dot is unchanged;
- a scale operand per the PTX selector rules: thread-id-a with byte-id 0, so
  lanes `%4 == 0` supply row `g` and lanes `%4 == 1` row `g+8`; thread-id-b
  with byte-id 0 for the column's own group.

The define is `DS4_CUDA_HAVE_MXF4`, tied by the Makefile to
`-gencode arch=compute_121a,code=sm_121a`. ds41 is a Spark-only family, so a
Blackwell-only path is acceptable here; on any other build the s8 arm is the
default and asking for the MXFP4 arm falls back to it with one line.

## Rounds

1. The s8 arm, scouted A/B against the scalar kernel on one binary with the
   switch, one fresh process per arm, at 12k, 106k and 300k. The scout
   established the direction; the record's campaign replaced its numbers.
2. The MXFP4 arm, same protocol, added beside the s8 arm.
3. The default flipped to MXFP4 on a Blackwell build and s8 elsewhere; the
   scalar kernel stays as the kill switch and for shapes the arms refuse.
4. Two independent reviews before the recorded campaign. They supplied the
   capture-time growth guard and the shape gate, and found three items that
   are fixed in what is committed here: the tiny-block threshold moved from
   2^-100 to the representable band (2^-122), the non-Blackwell fallback
   printing once instead of per call, and the switch comment no longer
   contradicting the runtime setter.

## Results

Two fresh processes per arm, 32 full-prefill frontiers from 2K to 64K, 128
generated tokens, zero reused prompt tokens
([record](benchmarks/ds41-indexer-arm-2026-11-08/README.md)):

| Metric | scalar | s8 | MXFP4 | MXFP4 vs scalar |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Prefill, mean of run means (tok/s) | 980.2 | 1,093.2 | 1,097.6 | +12.0% |
| Prefill at 64K (tok/s) | 891.9 | 1,095.8 | 1,104.4 | +23.8% |
| Prefill at 2K (tok/s) | 954.7 | 965.0 | 963.7 | +0.9% |
| Decode, automatic MTP (tok/s) | 35.3 | 36.2 | 36.1 | +2.3% |

Every completion is byte-identical across the three arms at all 32 frontiers
([receipts](benchmarks/ds41-indexer-arm-2026-11-08/receipts.json)). The gain
is a prefill term: decode moves +2.3% between arms, inside its own run-to-run
spread, and is not claimed.

The two tensor-core arms are within 1.2% of each other at every frontier and
+0.4% apart on the mean. The s8 arm carries the gain; MXFP4 is the served
default because it matches s8 inside the run-to-run spread and issues one
instruction per 64 dims against two.

## Numerics

Bit identity is not the gate (`AGENTS.md`). What is measured:

- the record's 192 completions are byte-identical across the three arms and
  both runs at all 32 frontiers (`receipts.json`);
- decode rate: no gain is claimed. The +2.3% between arms sits inside the
  per-frontier run-to-run spread, which is the arm's honest resolution.

The scalar-versus-arm difference is one summation tree (four block sums
against lane-in-then-butterfly). No per-position teacher-forced logits
surface exists for this family (the V4.1 route runs on `--gen-ids` and has no
`ds4_session`), so the receipt gate is generated content plus decode rate,
not NLL.

## Switch and files

- `DS4_DS41_IDX_MMA`: 0 scalar (kill switch), 1 s8 arm, 2 native MXFP4.
  Default 2 on a Blackwell build, 1 otherwise. Read once per process;
  `ds4-perf` may vary it (`crates/ds4-perf/src/knobs.rs`).
- `cuda/ds41_indexer_mma.cuh` (both arms), `cuda/ds41_indexer.cuh`
  (dispatch), `ds41_forward.h` and `ds4_ds41_forward.inc` (scratch growth
  before capture), `ds4_ds41_gpu.cuh` (include), `ds4_ds41_graph.inc`
  (capture note), `Makefile` (`ds4_cuda.o` dependency).

## Limits

- One host, one artifact, one serial text workload. Clock medians in the
  record sit above the documented 300-2200 MHz range; they are stated beside
  the numbers and are not comparable with other families.
- The tensor-core dispatch covers 32/64 heads x 128 dims; any other metadata
  shape takes the scalar kernel.
- The arms quantise q from its f32 form on every launch (one prep kernel over
  the scored rows); a caller that still holds the packed nibbles would not
  need it, and nothing in the port does yet.
