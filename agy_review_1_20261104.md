# Gemma-4 Effort & Priority Review (`2026-11-04`, commit `2165a6f3`)

Independent read-only audit of `/data/ds4-gemma4` (`feat/gemma4-family` at `2165a6f3`) comparing the proposed roadmap in [gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L2283-L2508) against the live C/CUDA/Rust implementation and measured GB10 / RTX 4070 SUPER profiles.

## 1. Cut or Defer Immediately (Sunk-Cost Traps)

### 1.1 Cut Phase 13 (`--n-cpu-moe` and `Unit 13.0` AVX2 Microbenchmark) Completely
- **Reference:** [gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L2482-L2508), [cpu_moe_agy_pane.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cpu_moe_agy_pane.md)
- **Why the math rules it out:**
  1. **Plain decode already fits 100% in 12 GiB VRAM on 4070 SUPER:** All `9.37 GiB` of trunk weights fit resident in VRAM at `ctx = 16384` (`10.16 GiB / 12 GiB` used, running at `20.95 tok/s` plain decode; [gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L1820-L1827)).
  2. **With `--mtp` (`+0.58 GiB`), the GPU active-expert slot cache beats CPU MoE by 4–8×:** At `4 GiB` (`--ssd-streaming`), [`ds4_gemma4_stream.inc`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_stream.inc) holds `2,189` of `3,840` expert slots in VRAM, achieves a **98.94% hit rate** (`250,746` hits / `2,694` misses across 4 benchmarks; [gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L1733-L1735)), and executes all 240 routed experts in **`9.29 ms/tok`** on GPU (`6.24 ms` gate+up + `3.05 ms` down).
  3. **CPU AVX2 VQ decode is 4–8× slower and adds 60 stream syncs/token:** Even an ideal multithreaded AVX2 `vpshufb`/`vpgatherdd` decoder with `64 KiB` L1-resident codebooks takes `38–55 ms/tok` at `rows = 1` and **`180–250 ms/step`** at `rows = 7` MTP verify (`48` unique experts/layer × 30 layers), plus 60 synchronous D2H/H2D transfers per token (`30 layers × (router D2H + hidden H2D)`), while breaking bit-identity (`relL2 = 0.0`) against [`g4_vq_gemv_kernel`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma_vq_decode.cuh#L50-L158).
- **Verdict:** Drop Phase 13 and do not spend effort building `Unit 13.0`.

### 1.2 Cut Phase 12.2 (`SLRU` Probationary/Protected Cache Policy)
- **Reference:** [gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L2421-L2428), [`gemma4_stream_Ensure`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_stream.inc#L469-L518)
- **Why:** Across the 4-prompt benchmark (`253,440` expert lookups), total bytes read from disk/cache are `4.92 GiB`, of which **`4.32 GiB` (`87.8%`) are mandatory first-touch cold misses** and only **`0.60 GiB` (`12.2%`) are eviction re-reads** (`98.94%` hit rate). Replacing the clock-hand FIFO/LRU in `gemma4_stream_Ensure` with SLRU cannot touch the `4.32 GiB` cold misses and can at best save a fraction of `0.60 GiB`.
- The actual `17.5 s` bottleneck in `--ssd-streaming` is calling synchronous `pread()` 6 times per expert into a pageable buffer in [`gemma4_stream_read`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_stream.inc#L355-L405) instead of copying from `s->model->map` into pinned memory (Phase 12.3).
- **Verdict:** Skip Phase 12.2 (`SLRU`); only do Phase 12.1 (CLI/Rust plumbing) and Phase 12.3 (`mmap` + pinned staging copy).

### 1.3 Defer Phase 4 (`--vq-profile` Consumer) to Last
- **Reference:** [gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L2358-L2377), [`g4_vq_gemv`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma_vq_decode.cuh#L160-L188)
- **Why:** `g4_vq_gemv` is `9.29 ms/step` (`19.8%` of 4070 decode) and only selects between `G4_VQ_WARP` and `G4_VQ_BLOCK` with `threads ∈ {128, 256, 512}`. Building CSV parsers, FFI bridges, and CLI flags for a few percent on batch-1 GEMV while GB10 prefill is 3.5× below floor and MTP verify has structural fallbacks is inverted priority.

### Priority 2: Fix the 4 MTP Drafter & Verify Fast-Path Bottlenecks (Why GB10 MTP Collapses from `18.53` to `8.53 tok/s` at `ctx=8192`)

On GB10 ([gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L1904-L1913)), `draft = 6` only reaches `20.21 tok/s` at `ctx = 1024` and collapses to **`8.53 tok/s` at `ctx = 8192`** (`54%` slower than plain decode at `18.53 tok/s`). Four concrete bugs/fallbacks in [`ds4_gemma4_mtp.inc`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_mtp.inc) and [`ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh) explain this:

#### 2.1 Drafter Attention Is Locked Out of `ds4_gpu_g4_attn_fast` by a Bogus `keys` Guard (8× Redundant Global KV Reads)
- In [`gemma4_mtp_step`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_mtp.inc#L265-L267), the drafter calls the slow unstaged oracle [`ds4_gpu_g4_attn`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L472-L480) (`4 layers × 6 drafts = 24` launches/round) with `keys = start`.
- Even if routed to [`ds4_gpu_g4_attn_fast`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L482-L488), line 487 rejects any non-zero `keys`:
  ```c
  if (!out || !q || !k || !v || !q->data || !k->data || !v->data || rows != 1 || keys || !scale || !max_seq) return 0;
  ```
- Yet [`g4_attn_fast_kernel`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma4_attn_fast.cuh#L47) **already implements `keys` properly**:
  ```cuda
  const uint32_t last = keys && keys <= position ? keys - 1u : position;
  ```
- Because of that `|| keys` rejection, on the drafter's full-attention layer (`il = 3`, `dim = 512`, `group = 8`), all 16 query heads independently stream the entire 8K KV cache from global memory using 2-byte scalar loads on every one of the 6 draft steps (`6 × 8×` redundant global memory reads per MTP step).
- **Action:** In [`ds4_gpu_g4_attn_fast`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L487), change `|| keys` to `|| (keys && keys > first + rows)`, and call `ds4_gpu_g4_attn_fast` in [`gemma4_mtp_step`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_mtp.inc#L265-L267) when `gemma4_attn_fast_env()` is enabled. This is a 5-line, 0-ULP bit-identical fix.

#### 2.2 Drafter Runs Unfused Serial RoPE and Separate `gate`/`up` Projections
- [`gemma4_mtp_step`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_mtp.inc#L259-L262) calls `ds4_gpu_g4_rms` + `ds4_gpu_g4_rope` 24 times per round instead of [`ds4_gpu_g4_rms_rope`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L446-L466).
- [`gemma4_mtp_step`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_mtp.inc#L278-L281) calls `ds4_gpu_gvq_linear` twice for `gate` and `up` instead of [`ds4_gpu_gvq_linear_many`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L350-L398) (24 extra launches and duplicate input quantize/reads per round).
- **Action:** Wire `ds4_gpu_g4_rms_rope` and `ds4_gpu_gvq_linear_many` into `gemma4_mtp_step`.

#### 2.3 Trunk Verify at `rows = 2..7` Hits `BN = 32` Prefill Padding Waste (`87.5%` Wasted FMAs)
- When `DS4_GEMMA4_VQ_PREFILL=1`, a 7-token verify step (`56` routes across `~48` unique experts per layer, almost all `n = 1` or `n = 2` tokens/expert) enters [`g4_moe_run_prefill`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L925-L931), which launches [`g4_vq_gemm_items_kernel`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma_vq_prefill.cuh#L231-L289) with `G4_VQP_BN = 32` ([cuda/gemma_vq_prefill.cuh](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma_vq_prefill.cuh#L52-L60)).
- For `n = 1..2`, **30–31 of the 32 token columns in every CTA are zero-padded**: 7 of 8 thread groups (`n_grp = tid & 7`) multiply zeros while paying full shared-memory staging overhead.
- **Action:** Per [AGENTS.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/AGENTS.md#L274-L277) (*"MTP verify widths are their own regime... Give direct kernels a token loop that reuses each weight load"*), either instantiate a narrow `BN = 4` variant of `g4_vq_gemm_items_kernel` for `rows <= 8` or give [`g4_vq_gemv_kernel`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma_vq_decode.cuh#L50-L158) a `1..4`-token loop sharing one weight decode.

#### 2.4 Unblock `DS4_GEMMA4_FULL_KV=fp8` With `--mtp`
- Currently rejected in [`ds4_gemma4_load`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4.c#L74111-L74117) because [`gemma4_mtp_step`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_mtp.inc#L254-L267) reads `g->kcache[29]` (which is `NULL` when `g->full_fp8` is set; `g->fkcache[29]` holds the FP8 cache).
- **Action:** Call [`ds4_gpu_g4_attn_fp8`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L506-L526) on drafter layer `il == 3` when `g->full_fp8` is enabled and remove the exclusion in `ds4_gemma4_load`.

### Priority 3: GB10 Long-Prompt Prefill (`283 tok/s` at 13.5K -> `>1,000 tok/s` Floor)

Per [AGENTS.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/AGENTS.md#L202-L216), prefill is priority #1 until every context segment clears `~1,000 tok/s`. On GB10, `13,515` tokens at `chunk = 2048` takes `47.8 s` (`283 tok/s`, [gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L1921-L1923)). Three bottlenecks dominate:

#### 3.1 Full-Layer Prefill Attention Has `rtile = 1` (Zero KV Reuse Across Query Rows, Scalar FP32 Warp Dots)
- In [`g4_attn_tile_kernel`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma4_attn_tile.cuh#L42-L131), the 5 full-attention layers (`dim = 512`, `group = 8`) set `rtile = (group == 8) ? 1u : 4u`. Every single query row streams the entire 8K–13.5K KV cache from DRAM/L2 with zero reuse across query rows, computing scalar FP32 warp dots. At `13.5K` context, full-layer attention is `>61%` of attention work.
- **Action:** Implement **Phase 8 (`cuda/gemma4_attn_mma.cuh`)** ([gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L2379-L2388)). Packing `r_tile × group` (`2 × 8 = 16` or `4 × 8 = 32` rows) into `mma.sync.aligned.m16n8k16.row.col.f32.bf16.bf16.f32` cuts full-layer KV memory traffic by `2–4×` and moves `Q·K^T` and `P·V` onto tensor cores.

#### 3.2 MoE Prefill (`g4_vq_gemm_items_kernel`) Thrashes L1 With the `64 KiB` Codebook and Uses Scalar FMA
- In [`g4_vq_gemm_items_kernel`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma_vq_prefill.cuh#L231-L289), each CTA allocates `wa[4456] + wb[4456] + xs[2080] + s_res[128] = 43.3 KiB` of shared memory. On GB10 (`sm_121`), that leaves `<21 KiB` of L1 for the `64 KiB` E4M3 codebook (`m.book`), forcing random codebook gather misses out of L1 into L2 while computing via scalar `fmaf`.
- **Action:**
  - *Immediate (<1 hr):* In the `paired` (`gate`+`up`) path of [`g4_vq_gemm_items_kernel`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/gemma_vq_prefill.cuh#L244-L282), reuse a single `wa[4456]` shared buffer sequentially (decode `gate` -> accumulate `acc_a` -> `__syncthreads()` -> decode `up` -> accumulate `acc_b`) and set `cudaFuncAttributePreferredSharedMemoryCarveout` to `25%` (`~25.9 KiB` SMEM / `~102 KiB` L1) so the `64 KiB` codebook stays resident in L1.
  - *Follow-up:* Port the `mma.sync.aligned.m16n8k32.row.col.f32.e4m3.e4m3.f32` tensor-core loop from [`cuda/ds41_vq_prefill_mma.cuh`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/cuda/ds41_vq_prefill_mma.cuh) to Gemma-4's `k_Step = 256, v_len = 4` layout.

#### 3.3 30 Synchronous Host-Device Roundtrips per Chunk/Token in [`ds4_gpu_g4_moe_run`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L859-L1019)
- Every layer calls `ds4_gpu_tensor_read(selected, ...)` (synchronous D2H `cudaMemcpy`, draining the stream), sorts routes on CPU, and runs 4 synchronous `ds4_gpu_tensor_write` H2D uploads (`m->order`, `m->tokpair`, `m->qmat`, `m->items` × 2).
- **Action:** Per [AGENTS.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/AGENTS.md#L242-L244) (*"No host syncs on the hot path... per-layer routing readback plus an item upload cost ~1.3 ms per decoded token"*), move the `128`-expert counting sort and `ds4_gvq_item` construction onto a single-block GPU kernel (`128` threads = `128` experts) when `g->stream == NULL` (all weights resident in VRAM).

#### 3.4 Fix `chunk = 8192` Router `cuBLAS` Failure ([gemma4-port-handoff.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/gemma4-port-handoff.md#L1918-L1924))
- Inspect [`ds4_gpu_g4_router_proj`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_gpu.cuh#L468-L470) / `glmvq_gemm` workspace sizing at `rows = 8192` so GB10 can run `--prefill-chunk-size 8192`.

### Priority 4: Serving Hardening & 4070 `--ssd-streaming` Plumbing

   - Killing an `open-grok` request mid-flight leaves the session/graph slot wedged so subsequent requests fail with HTTP 503 `"no session graph fits beside the batch banks"`. Fix cancellation cleanup before relying on `ds4-server` for interactive agent loops.
2. **Add the SWA Ring-Resume Guard in [`gemma4_sync_keep`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_session.inc#L39-L77):**
   - Currently `gemma4_sync_keep` rewinds to `common` even when `s->checkpoint.len - common > G4_WINDOW` (`1024`), where the SWA ring has already overwritten those positions ([docs/gemma4-vq.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/docs/gemma4-vq.md#L247-L249), [AGENTS.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/AGENTS.md#L116-L122)). Clamp or fall back to snapshot/zero when the rewind exceeds `G4_WINDOW`.
3. **Fix the 3 Bugs Blocking `ds4-server --ssd-streaming --mtp` on 4070 SUPER:**
   - **Bug A ([ds4.c:74690-74693](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4.c#L74690-L74693)):** When `opt->ssd_streaming` is set, the unit compiler calls `glm53_stream_spans(&e->weights, &sl_spans)` instead of `gemma4_stream_spans(&e->gemma4, &sl_spans)`.
   - **Bug B ([ds4.c:74554-74568](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4.c#L74554-L74568)):** When `e->mtp_ready` is true, `ds4_create_execution_engine_with_options` calls `ds4_gpu_set_model_map_range` for the MTP shard, overwriting `g_model_host_base` and breaking trunk `ds4_gpu_tensor_write` calls.
   - **Bug C ([crates/ds4-core/src/ssd.rs](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/crates/ds4-core/src/ssd.rs)):** Relax the single-file GLM-5.3 guard so `ds4-server --ssd-streaming` accepts Gemma-4 release directories ([docs/gemma4-vq.md](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/docs/gemma4-vq.md#L244-L246)).
   - **Fast I/O path ([`gemma4_stream_read`](file:///data/ds4-gemma4/ds4_gemma4_stream.inc#L355-L405)):** When `!s->cold && s->model && s->model->map`, copy directly from `(const uint8_t *)s->model->map + offset` into a 408 KiB `cudaMallocHost` staging buffer + `cudaMemcpyAsync` instead of 6 `pread()` syscalls into pageable heap memory (Phase 12.1 + 12.3 only).
